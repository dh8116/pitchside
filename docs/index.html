What it does

A 30-day challenge, run from the chat you already have open.

Morning check-ins

Today's one outcome at 8am your time, and it remembers what you committed to.

Posts in your voice

"Day N of 30 with Pitchside" drafts, one line per thought. Reply "post it" and it publishes to X, LinkedIn or Bluesky.

Outreach coaching

Who to target, cold email copy, send timing, deliverability and follow-ups.

Launch checks

Pressure-tests launch plans and landing copy, and looks up competitors.

Set it up in about 15 minutes

Each person or team runs their own copy, so your conversations stay on your server. No coding needed.

  1. Get a free model keyFrom Google AI Studio or Groq.
  2. Fill in your settingsThe setup page explains every option, generates secrets and gives you a finished .env file. Nothing leaves your browser.
  3. Deploy itFork the repo and deploy it on Railway or Render, or run npm start on your laptop to try the browser chat first.
  4. Connect a channel and say hiWhatsApp's free test number, Twilio SMS, iMessage via BlueBubbles, or email via Postmark. Channel guides.

Start your 30 days.

Free to run, open source, and yours to change.
